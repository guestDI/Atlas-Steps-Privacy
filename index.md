# Privacy Policy — Personal Atlas

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Personal Atlas ("the app") turns the steps you walk into a virtual journey around the world. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: **your data stays on your device, and we don't collect any of it.**

## What the app reads

- **Step count from Apple Health.** With your permission, the app reads your step count to move your journey forward. It only reads steps. It never writes to Apple Health, and it never reads other health data.

## What stays on your device

Your steps, your balance, your journey progress, your postcards and your settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them, and we have no servers that hold them. There is no account, and no sign-up.

If you use iCloud or computer backups for your device, your backup may include the app's data, under Apple's backup rules and controls. The app itself does not upload it anywhere.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect, sell or share personal information.
- It does not use your location.
- It does not use advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.
- It does not use health data for advertising or marketing.

## Internet use

The app downloads new countries (journey maps and postcard images) from our content server at `packs.nexlifypal.com`. This is a plain download. The app sends no account, health or step information. As with any web request, the server and its hosting provider (Cloudflare) can see your IP address in their standard technical logs. We do not use it to identify you.

## Notifications

If you allow them, the app sends a quiet notification from your device when your steps can reach a new place. These notifications are created locally on your device. No notification service receives your data, and you can turn them off at any time in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

## Purchases

The optional Pro unlock is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only learns from Apple whether Pro is unlocked.

## Your choices and your data

- **Delete it:** use *Reset atlas* in the app's Settings to erase your progress, or delete the app. Apple Health records are never touched.
- **Revoke access:** you can stop the app reading steps at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Because the app collects nothing, we don't expect material changes.
